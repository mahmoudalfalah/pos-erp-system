# POS ERP Conventions

Always use `pnpm` (never `npm` or `npx`).

Before creating or modifying code, check the references below and read **only** the files whose topic is involved in your current task—skip unrelated ones.

## References

- **Files, folders, naming, types, or configs:** `references/architecture-and-naming.md`
- **Domain types, DTOs, mappers, or validators:** `references/contracts-and-mappers.md`
- **Write operations (Server Actions & Services):** `references/mutations.md`
- **Read operations (Queries & Query Builders):** `references/queries.md`
- **Unit tests, fixtures, & mocking:** `references/testing-and-mocking.md`
